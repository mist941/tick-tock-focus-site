Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-23

Summary

  • TickTockFocus doesn't collect any personal data.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer. The extension makes no network requests.
  • When you remove the extension, Chrome opens a short feedback page on this website. Nothing about you is sent with it.
  • No data is sold or transferred to anyone.
  • Your routines and settings stay in Chrome's own storage. If you turn on Chrome Sync, Google syncs them between your browsers.

Scope

This policy covers the TickTockFocus Chrome extension and this website, ticktockfocus.com.

What is stored and where

The extension keeps a small amount of data in Chrome's extension storage so that it can work. It never sends this data anywhere itself; the only way it leaves your browser is Chrome Sync, if you turn it on.

Permissions and why they're needed

storage
Saves your routines and settings and the counters for the rating prompt, and remembers the running timer so it keeps going when you close the popup or restart Chrome.
alarms
Wakes the extension at the end of each step, even when the popup is closed.
notifications
Shows a notification when each step ends and when the whole routine is finished.
offscreen
Plays the end-of-step sound from a hidden extension page, because the extension's background service worker can't play audio. It only plays sound files bundled with the extension.

The extension has no host permissions and no content scripts, so it can't read or change any website.

What the extension doesn't do

  • It makes no network requests. Chrome Sync, if you turn it on, is handled by Chrome itself. The only pages it opens on its own are its welcome page after you install it (part of the extension, not this website) and ticktockfocus.com/uninstall.html after you remove it. Links in Settings, on the welcome page and in the rating prompt (Rate on Chrome Web Store) open only when you click them.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or tracking of any kind.
  • No ads.
  • No cookies.
  • No account or sign-in.
  • No access to the websites you visit, your browsing history, or the content of any page.

Your control over your data

  • Delete a routine: in the Routines tab, open the routine's ⋯ menu and choose Delete. You can undo it for a few seconds. The routine is removed from the extension's storage and, when Chrome Sync is on, from your other synced browsers too.
  • Stop syncing: turn off Chrome Sync in Chrome's settings. Routines and settings you save after that stay in this browser.
  • Clear the running timer: pressing Stop clears its state. It's also cleared when a run finishes.
  • Uninstall: removing the extension deletes its local data (chrome.storage.local) from this browser, including the number of finished routines and your rating answer. To remove synced routines from your other browsers too, delete them in the extension before you uninstall it. After removal, Chrome opens ticktockfocus.com/uninstall.html, with links to email feedback or report a bug. You can simply close it; nothing is sent unless you choose to write to us.

This website

ticktockfocus.com is a static site. It uses no cookies, no analytics, and no JavaScript, and it doesn't load anything from other websites: styles and images come from this site, and text uses your device's own fonts.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data, such as your IP address, to deliver the site and keep it secure. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Links to other sites, such as the Chrome Web Store and GitHub, lead to pages covered by those sites' own privacy policies.

Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The use of information by TickTockFocus complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The extension uses the data described above only to provide its features. The data is never sold, never used for advertising or to determine creditworthiness, and the developer never has access to it.
